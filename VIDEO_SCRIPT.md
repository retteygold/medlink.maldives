# MedLink Maldives – Demo Video Script (60–90 seconds)

## Opening (0–10s)

- **Visual**: Clean splash screen with MedLink logo and tagline “Connecting you to healthcare since 2019”
- **Voiceover**: “Finding the right doctor or hospital in the Maldives has never been easier. Meet MedLink Maldives – your mobile-first healthcare directory.”

## Core Features Demo (10–45s)

- **Visual**: Home screen with greeting and search bar
- **Action**: Type “cardiologist” → dropdown suggestions appear
- **Voiceover**: “Search for doctors or hospitals instantly. Results appear as you type – no waiting.”

- **Visual**: Tap a doctor → detailed profile with qualifications, hospital, ratings
- **Voiceover**: “View detailed profiles, including specialties, qualifications, and patient ratings.”

- **Visual**: Switch language toggle from English to Dhivehi
- **Voiceover**: “Available in both English and Dhivehi – designed for everyone in the Maldives.”

- **Visual**: Browse hospitals by location (Male’, Hulhumale’, Islands, Resorts)
- **Voiceover**: “Filter by location and services – find 24/7 emergency care, pharmacies, and labs near you.”

## Community & Admin (45–60s)

- **Visual**: Community Q&A – ask a health question, view answers
- **Voiceover**: “Ask health questions and get answers from the community – all in one place.”

- **Visual**: Quick flash of admin dashboard (manage doctors/hospitals)
- **Voiceover**: “For administrators: easily manage listings, reviews, and keep information up to date.”

## PWA & Closing (60–75s)

- **Visual**: Install prompt on mobile → app icon on home screen
- **Voiceover**: “Install it as a mobile app – works offline and loads instantly.”

- **Visual**: Final screen with live URL and QR code
- **Voiceover**: “Ready to experience better healthcare access? Visit medlink-maldives.vercel.app today.”

## On-Screen Text Overlays

- “Instant search”
- “English & Dhivehi”
- “Ratings & Reviews”
- “PWA – Install as app”
- “Admin dashboard”
- “Community Q&A”

## Music/Style

- Upbeat, clean background music
- Fast-paced cuts (2–3s per scene)
- Mobile device framing throughout
- Highlight taps and transitions

## Call to Action

- Final screen: “Live now – medlink-maldives.vercel.app”
- QR code for quick mobile access
- “Built with React, Supabase, and Vercel”

---

**Total runtime**: ~75 seconds  
**Target audience**: General public, healthcare seekers, clinic admins  
**Platforms**: Social media (Instagram/Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts), website homepage
